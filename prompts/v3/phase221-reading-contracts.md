### The reading argument

The final deliverable is a complete beginner-readable scientific explanation in each language. Organize by research question within the established12 managed sections, not by16 compulsory headings or semantic-ID cards. Research design and reasoning receive the greatest explanatory effort, methods/concepts/measures the next, and results the next. This is not a fixed word ratio or permission to delete any result, condition, evidence, limitation or obligation. A brief overview only opens the full analysis.

For each research-question thread, explain the actual question and existing evidence gap, the core idea, the design choices, the data and stepwise workflow, method roles, findings and what remains unresolved. State why each experiment or analysis is needed; for each meaningful step connect its input, operation and output to the next step and to the evidence it can provide or the alternative explanation it can address. Explain what remains indistinguishable. Merely arranging data/method/result fields or adding “first, then, finally” does not explain these connections.

Keep three design-rationale categories distinct: what the authors explicitly report; an analyst's source-supported explanation of why a choice may serve the question; and a motive the source does not report. Mark the second as interpretation and the third as unknown. Do not invent author intent for narrative fluency. The core idea is the conceptual solution, not a list of named tools.

For every key method, explain its basic principle and the problem it addresses, applicable assumptions and output meaning; then explain this paper's actual input/objects, operation/sequence, reported parameters/thresholds/software, output and upstream/downstream use. Explain why that use fits the design only to the extent supported. State what kind of inference the method permits and what it cannot establish in this study. For concepts and measures give the supported definition, operationalization, unit/scale, study use and interpretation boundary. Necessary formulas need source, symbols, intuition and purpose. Missing foundation, sense or computational information stays a named gap, not a textbook invention. Main-note explanations must remain sufficient even when method/concept nodes offer further reading.

For every important finding, connect the originating experiment/analysis, question, comparison, conditions, measure and analysis population to the direction, effect size and uncertainty. Explain whether and how it supports, challenges or refines the proposed account, its role in later analysis, and which stronger claim does not follow. Preserve null, conflicting or adverse results when relevant. Discussion must interpret evidence, alternatives and contribution instead of repeating the abstract or listing results. Conclusions distinguish answered questions, remaining questions, theoretical/method/practice implications and the conditions and further validation they require. Do not turn association, prediction, proxy outcomes or descriptive comparisons into causal, intervention or clinical proof.

### Sixteen substantive obligations

Each obligation must be expressed in both complete reading drafts or have a specific supported applicability/material-limit reason with its effect. These are scientific-reading duties, not mandatory display headings. In records use only the statuses allowed by the injected schema; assessed, not_applicable and not_assessable have different meanings. “Not reported” describes absent source information, not an invented fourth status. Accessible but unprocessed material is not unavailable or inapplicable.

| --- | --- |
| SR-01 Paper position | Explain the paper's field position and related directions, separating author positioning from independently checked external context. Do not assert novelty from a title or unsearched background. |
| SR-02 Bounded reading summary | Provide the separately attributed one-sentence problem/approach/finding/limit statement above. |
| SR-03 Research question | State the specific question, why it matters, the prior insufficiency and any testable hypothesis without inventing one. |
| SR-04 Background route | Explain how prior approaches led to the question and what this paper inherits or changes, with actual source attribution. |
| SR-05 Core pain points | Explain the concrete problem, the authors' proposed explanation and the evidence for it, including unsupported parts. |
| SR-06 Core idea | Explain the conceptual move that addresses the gap and why it differs from simply naming methods. |
| SR-07 Method overview | Connect input, full workflow, tools, outputs, assumptions and applicable cost/feasibility constraints; mark unreported costs rather than invent them. |
| SR-08 Module breakdown | For each essential module explain necessity, responsibility, input, operation, output, adjacent-step dependence and evidence limits. |
| SR-09 Essential formulas | Explain formulas necessary to understand the argument, source and symbols, intuition and use. If none is needed give the specific applicability reason; missing required definitions remain gaps. |
| SR-10 Analysis–claim chain | Connect each important experiment/analysis to its question, comparison, conditions, quantitative finding, figure/table/evidence and allowed conclusion. |

### Repair ownership and acceptance boundary

Writer repairs language, explanatory organization and omissions that the unchanged complete basis already supports. Missing scientific foundation, ambiguous terminology sense, unsupported relation, quantity identity or author-rationale evidence requires a bounded Integrator request identifying the unit, source/location, missing layer and effect. Controller handles transport, mapping and deterministic rendering defects; it must not author missing science. Preserve unchanged supported text and record the required revision according to the assigned schema rather than simultaneously returning incompatible output kinds.

For both Writers and the Verifier, the six independent content standards are: (1) design choices and linked steps explain how the question is answered; (2) method principles, actual use, assumptions and output meanings are understandable; (3) conditioned results form an evidence-to-conclusion argument; (4) no substantive source/visual/obligation meaning is omitted or falsely qualified; (5) critique, transfer, connections and candidates have support and correct attribution; (6) both complete final language drafts are coherent and scientifically consistent. No result-rich section compensates for a deficient design explanation.

Scientific parity, content completeness, reading quality, native presentation and publication remain separate. Matching IDs, a complete heading list, word counts, fluent prose, many passing items or model agreement cannot establish any missing scientific meaning. Runtime hashes/owned receipts establish association, not scientific truth. Native Obsidian behavior requires separately supplied actual observation; text review cannot self-certify it. User understanding feedback does not transfer expert verification responsibility to the user.
