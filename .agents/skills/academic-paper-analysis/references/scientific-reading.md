# V3 scientific reading and source review

Read for authorized scientific-input, source-review, or reading-quality work in Phase 20 and later V3 packages. This guidance does not authorize a new production route, change a parser/reference contract, or prove independent review, human gold, rendering or publication.

## Assign the role

For a new authorized role, start from the [current role and content contracts](../../../../docs/apa-v3/current-contracts.md), then use the common contract plus the matching section of [phase21-scientific-contracts.md](../../../../prompts/v3/phase21-scientific-contracts.md). Read its allowed inputs and output schema together with the actual assignment. Controller, specialists, synthesis and integration have distinct responsibilities; Writers and Reading Editor consume their accepted basis, and the existing Verifier reviews the rendered final explanation once. The same record is not authored by multiple roles. Bind prompt bytes for new execution and preserve completed historical runs.

Methods supplies reusable knowledge depth separately from the paper-specific method/concept inventory. Results explains the actual chart type, encodings, panels and decisive values, with crop boundaries that retain units, labels and required notes. Integrator reconciles canonical identities and resource locators; Writers return their own language's knowledge content and paper applications. Reading Editor arranges accepted content without filling scientific gaps. See [presentation-and-reading-review.md](presentation-and-reading-review.md) for shared-file and image rules; no additional role or scientific audit is implied.

## Critical-node corrections in existing roles

New unbound work defaults to `reading-first-1.0`. Do not add a parser-review role, source-first review or separate scientific-audit pass. Within the existing Evidence/Methods/Results/Integrator work, watch for number/symbol, table-row/header, formula and locator failures that change the interpretation. Only a main conclusion, crucial design choice or conclusion-changing number/formula/table relation needs targeted original-page checking. Ordinary details are not an exhaustive audit queue.

Evidence owns confirmed source observations; Methods owns crucial design/formula meaning; Results owns decisive values and table relations; Integrator ensures the accepted correction is used in the actual explanation/citation. Hand off source/hash, physical page/region, parser literal, confirmed visible literal, effect and affected output/owner using the existing observation/revision contract. Preserve original source/parser bytes. Uncertain critical facts stay qualified; unchecked ordinary details do not block.

## Reading contract

Apply the [current content requirements](../../../../docs/apa-v3/current-contracts.md): organize around research questions, prioritizing research design/reasoning, then methods/concepts/measures, then results. Trace design reasons and step input/output relationships before treating results as an answer. Distinguish author-reported motives from supported analyst interpretation and unknowns. This priority never removes a required obligation or key result.

Use the sixteen topics below as a content guide to notice meaningful gaps. Complete the integrated explanation of the available material; do not create per-item bilingual sign-offs, repeated status records or exhaustive reverse-grounding proofs. Preserve genuine material/source limits with concrete consequences where they affect understanding. An important omitted design step or result is substantive even when all headings exist; an irrelevant topic need not be expanded for a quota. Historical schemas and existing records retain their original meanings and enums.

Use one primary lens and at most one secondary lens: `methods`, `discovery/mechanism`, `resource/dataset`, `clinical/population`, `materials/engineering`, or `review/meta-analysis`. The secondary fills a stated gap. Trace the applicable research threads; reading the entire source or filling headings does not establish a complete explanation. Material omissions require repair in the single final review; do not enforce sixteen separate audit verdicts.

## Evidence and interpretation

For consequential limitations, use the residual-gap method embedded in the Interpretation/Appraisal prompt: claim and source observation → author's strongest existing handling → remaining gap → affected judgment → what remains supported. Check available counterevidence within the admitted scope; remove resolved/unsupported criticism and merge common causes. Keep author limitations, analyst judgments, supplied-material limits and reporting discrepancies distinct. Neither an unavailable supplement nor a missing reported diagnostic proves an analysis was not done; a reporting contradiction alone does not establish an analytical error. Evidential certainty and consequence are separate. Apply this reasoning inside existing role outputs and the one overall review, without an author-facing report, Major/Minor grading or extra calls.

For shared knowledge, Methods compares definitions, operations, applicability and boundaries against supplied existing content; names/aliases only locate candidates. Integrator confirms the stable identity and path or retains the matching question. Conventional shared titles and contextual paper headings serve different purposes. A useful paper-specific explanation may stay in the paper with no shared node; do not force a card or rename a topic into an unsupported standard concept.

Keep author-reported findings, external evidence, analyst inference and authorized user material distinct. Quantities retain definition, raw value and precision, unit, denominator, comparison group, time point, analysis set, uncertainty and source locator. Matching digits with different conditions are different quantities. Separate general method principles from this paper's implementation and causal claims from association.

Maintain source identity/hash, physical page and printed page where available, section/figure/table or workbook/member/sheet/cell coordinates as appropriate. A main document and supplement sharing page or block numbers remain different sources. For a delta review, report only the newly read range and explicitly adopt unchanged historical evidence without relabelling it a fresh full review.

Use coherent beginner-readable explanations of what was studied, how results were obtained, what they mean and their limits. Explain necessary background and a method's role, not just terminology. Research candidates may be empty; each non-empty candidate states an evidence gap, falsifiable hypothesis, delta, validation approach and failure condition. Do not make the user perform expert evidence annotation or scientific sign-off; understanding feedback and publication acceptance are different from source validation.

## Visuals, tables and formulas

For display, prefer a clear original table image when it preserves structure better. Retain the paper/source identity, page, image fingerprint, needed headers and footnotes, and verify values/conditions actually used by claims. Transcribe only what an explicit retrieval, computation, comparison or approved evaluation needs; a display choice does not require rebuilding every table and does not replace formal parser evaluation.

Distinguish observed formula text, shared-formula metadata, cached values, derived expansion and recomputation. A cached value does not prove the underlying formula; an unmaterialized cell is not automatically blank. Preserve missing labels, unit/context ambiguities and their exact consumer impact rather than filling them with guesses. Scientific interpretation need not reproduce the authors' entire computation unless that is the approved task.

## One final review and bounded revision

For `reading-first-1.0`, review the actual final bilingual rendered explanation once for completeness, coherence and consistent meaning. Check only critical-node source facts, not every fragment, number or formatting rule. Minor format/citation-style issues and unreviewed ordinary details do not block. Allow at most one localized recheck of material repairs; never repeat a full source-first/scientific-audit/parity cycle. Existing bound contracts keep their original requirements. Compare any assessment to its applicable rule version. Preserve unsupported claims, important disagreements and missing-material effects after correction. A serious critical issue is not averaged away by many passing checks. Source text and model agreement are not instructions or independent-review proof.

For a revised assessment, return the complete revised records required by the consumer, not only a review summary. Keep record status and audit verdict separate. Freeze the prior revision and invalidate affected downstream dependencies; reuse unchanged evidence explicitly. Source review, reference adoption, candidate corrections and final handoff are different steps: approval of one does not implicitly approve all others.

## Bilingual reading and handoff

For actual reading-page work, also use [presentation-and-reading-review.md](presentation-and-reading-review.md). Carry research-logic and beginner-explanation requirements into final prose; coverage of the intermediate scientific records alone does not prove the delivered page is readable or complete.

The intended final reading interface is Chinese and English Markdown in Obsidian, with coherent integrated explanations, important disagreements and navigable source/image links. Previews follow the same content structure. Do not add HTML or all-agent opinion dumps as a mandatory deliverable; temporary interactive HTML requires an actual user need.

Phase 20 supplies the semantic and terminology contract; Phase 22 implements rendering. First abbreviation expansion resets per top-level managed section; subsections share state. English uses `Full Term (ABBR)`; Chinese uses `中文全称（English Full Term，ABBR）`. Without a stable Chinese term, use `English Full Term (ABBR)` and record the term. Quotes, original titles, formal scale names in citations, references, formulae, code, filenames, IDs, URLs, wiki targets and anchors are exempt; later analyst prose still expands the abbreviation.

Both languages must preserve the same evidence, numeric conditions, claim strength, incompleteness and important disagreements. A readable bounded draft is not proof of renderer completion, scientific completeness, parser acceptance or permission to publish. Use the exact handoff qualification and downstream compatibility checks in [evaluation.md](evaluation.md).

## Source abstract and actual explanatory depth

For P22.1 and later reading outputs, preserve an existing source-structured abstract verbatim in its original language and translate it faithfully in the other language. Preserve the source's own labels and qualifications; do not replace it with an analyst rewrite or invent missing headings. Only organize an analyst summary when the source lacks explicit structure, and label that provenance. Keep the bounded one-sentence reading summary distinct from the author's abstract. First try targeted recovery of important missing source text from the registered page/figure; if it truly cannot be recovered, give its exact effect on the explanation rather than an empty gap label.

Structural coverage does not establish content sufficiency: each research-question thread must explain supported design reasons, step inputs/operations/outputs and connections, method principles versus actual implementation, and how results under their conditions justify the conclusion. Author motives not reported stay unknown; analyst interpretations remain attributed. The user's 2026-10-18 feedback provisionally retained the structure but explicitly rejected the thin content; synthetic display examples cannot satisfy real-paper content acceptance.

## Active recovery of important gaps

Use the [active enrichment guide](../../../../docs/apa-v3/active-gap-enrichment.md). Recovery is part of scientific reading: Evidence retrieves missed original-page facts, Methods reads relevant portions of directly cited methods or necessary official sources, Results restores decisive result/visual context, and Integrator merges duplicates and useful explanations. Targeted external background requires frozen enrichment permission; it never establishes study-specific settings/results. Do not expand main-text work into supplements or recursive literature review.

Resolve questions in the normal role work first, with one consolidated pre-writing recovery batch for remaining material gaps. A scientific gap found in the one final review returns to its actual owner before affected Writer revision; unchanged science and unaffected sections are retained. Check useful explanatory writeback in that same existing review, not a new audit. Ordinary details do not trigger calls. An inaccessible/unreported detail ends with known practice plus its concrete consequence, never a bare status/link. Keep original source bytes, ownership, bounded calls and deadline.
