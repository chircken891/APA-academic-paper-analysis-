## Recover the reader's requirements

The user-confirmed APA priority is research design and reasoning first, methods/concepts/measures second, results third. Organize by research question and explain why each design choice and step is needed, how inputs/outputs connect and what the evidence establishes; methods enable this understanding and results test it. This is an explanatory priority, not a length ratio or permission to omit results, conditions or any of the16 obligations. See the [current content requirements](../../../../docs/apa-v3/current-contracts.md).

Carry forward the accepted V2/V2.5 and later reading goals when changing a V3 page: explain the research problem, design reasons, data/steps, intermediate outputs, findings, interpretation and limits as a connected argument. Distinguish method principles from this paper's implementation. Explain necessary concepts in context; spelling out an acronym is not an explanation.

Preserve established managed identities and independent Personal Notes; use the sixteen reading topics as a content guide, without per-topic signing. The2026-10-20 user revision permits omitting or minimizing the visible Evidence, Relation Ledger and Coverage Report modules while retaining their complete retrievable records and consequential limitations in the reading flow. An overview guides entry; it does not replace the complete analysis. Do not convert every semantic unit into a separate heading/card or expose all role opinions. Important disagreements, author/analyst attribution and consequential uncertainty stay understandable in the reading flow.

## See a complete example early

Before completing peripheral consumers, preserve and show a complete bilingual example within the allowed scope. Record what was actually shown, any feedback, the affected area and revision owner in the phase's existing feedback record.

No response is not approval; preserve an honest awaiting/no-feedback state while independent reversible work continues. Do not repeatedly request permission already supplied. Early examples guide layout/content organization; later real-paper acceptance follows the applicable approved contract. Native presentation requirements also follow that bound scope; record only actual observations and retain `not_run` when none were performed.

## Preserve meaning while improving display

For new functional deliveries explicitly bound with `presentationMode:'reading-editor'`, the dedicated Reading Editor follows the source-language Writer and Translation (in legacy bindings, the source-first bilingual Writers) and precedes rendering. It consumes the accepted `views`, original `view_receipts`, scientific `context`, `sources` and merged knowledge/resource `presentation`; scientific paragraphs and ownership do not change. Build the inventory from these supplied records, not only scattered mentions in the prose. One bilingual output adds a linked overview, selective section takeaways, exact-quote emphasis, subheadings and an independent methods/concepts inventory. Bindings with `presentation_profile:'reading-editor-1.1'` also attach figure reading notes and short figure titles, and choose the staged research map's nodes and principal findings. The renderer supplies numbered hierarchy and stable navigation; under 1.1 it uses fixed module titles, three heading levels (subheadings become bold lead-ins), folded contents, catalogue entries and original captions, and places the map after the Writer's study-design overview. Bindings with no presentation profile keep the 1.0 layout. Use [the interface](../../../../docs/apa-v3/reading-first-obsidian.md) and the exact `Reading Editor` section of [the active prompt](../../../../prompts/v3/reading-first-contracts.md), not a parallel writing workflow. Existing Writer-only bindings retain their behavior.

The inventory lists each essential method, measure or concept with its supported principle and actual application in this study. An entry may remain unlinked when it has no established reusable identity; supplied `knowledge_ids` must be registered and both languages use the same per-entry concept set, including empty sets. Keep contextual explanations too. Overview and takeaway text must preserve consequential conditions and uncertainty. Exact emphasis spans must avoid links, formulas, code, existing bold and overlapping fragments. Nature/Humanizer's objection to empty labels does not prohibit meaningful bold, numbered hierarchy, tables, lists or callouts. Reading usefulness takes precedence over mechanically restrained prose formatting.

One independent method, concept or chart type means one identity and one physical bilingual knowledge file. Use names/aliases for candidate discovery and confirmed stable IDs/canonical keys for reuse; preserve existing paths. New filenames use clear Chinese canonical names without paper IDs, hashes or language suffixes. Both paper languages navigate to the same file's respective language section. Share formulas, figures, sources and the paper-application index within that file. Do not split by language or duplicate existing library nodes for a new paper. In a standalone preview, render the existing node's merged candidate at its registered path; never change the formal vault or erase human content. Keep historical candidates without using them as competing new entry points.

New Reading Editor bindings use `reading-editor-1.2` and the separately frozen [overlay](../../../../prompts/v3/reading-editor-1.2.md) alongside the unchanged base prompt. Keep every registered step in the map, select important findings and limits, and link every omitted finding to its section after the map. Existing 1.0 and 1.1 bindings retain their own profiles. New configured previews use verified historical knowledge text for referenced existing nodes and labeled navigation pages for older papers; those preview resources are not publication outputs.

Methods provides the generic teaching material: definition, principle, formulas/symbols, a clearly labelled example where useful, applicability, limitations and common confusions. The paper's inventory explains why and how this study uses the method and what that means for interpreting its results. Integrator reconciles identities and background versus paper facts; the source Writer supplies its language section and targeted paper changes, and Translation supplies the other language section. Reading Editor arranges these real supplied outputs, without inventing missing scientific content. An important gap returns to its content owner under the authorized targeted-research policy, budget and original deadline.

Results identifies actual figure types and composite panels; `visual.chart_types` connects them to reusable method nodes. Put only short chart-type labels next to the paper figure, with fuller reading instructions in the shared node. Crop original PDF figures, tables and formulas to relevant content, preserving axes, legends, units, panel labels, required headers and footnotes. Captions and source locators remain complete beside the image. Record physical page, crop, rotation and source fingerprint; preserve originals. Split multiple figures on a page and keep continuation tables connected. Use a fuller image when the crop boundary is uncertain. Do not redraw scientific content to improve appearance.

For section-specific writing, consult only the relevant entries of the [module reference](../../../../docs/apa-v3/scientific-writing-modules.md). Keep the workflow instruction short: when enabled in the repository configuration, Nature organizes the module and Humanizer improves its expression in the same Writer call. Disabled companion guidance is not injected. The full module guide stays outside the request body; no extra checklist or review stage is introduced.

- Use readable titles and link labels. Stable semantic IDs, offsets and hashes support traceability; they should not form a wall of visible body text. Changing display must preserve important supported meaning and identify which consumers need adaptation.
- A title property does not necessarily replace the visible filename, and hiding properties through global settings is not a per-note solution. Optional CSS must not be the only thing preventing loss of essential text or navigation.
- Treat valid expanded terminology as atomic; avoid repeated full forms and nested expansion. Quotes, formulas, code and link targets remain protected. Repair ambiguous meaning through the proper content owner, not a global bracket-removal expression.
- One visible quantity may represent equivalent storage formats when its definition, population, denominator, unit, time and uncertainty retain their actual meaning. Matching numeric values alone does not equate populations, denominators, units, times or uncertainty. Never remove precision/conditions merely to make a sentence shorter.
- Present actual source figures/tables/formulas with their original order, numbering, captions and required headers/footnotes. Link to supported findings and evidence. An inventory label is not a displayed figure; an available source needing asset derivation must be repaired within scope or remain blocked, not relabelled unavailable to pass.
- Fold detailed supporting evidence where useful, while keeping the main result and any limitation that changes its interpretation directly visible. Folded material must remain reachable, searchable where required and readable when expanded.

## Review the final delivered representation

Shared cards use supported conventional names; the paper may use a more specific application heading. Confirm identity through Methods/Integrator before deterministic reuse: names/aliases discover candidates, while stable IDs/canonical keys identify the accepted target. Name-only collisions return for disambiguation rather than silently merge or duplicate. An inventory entry with substantive explanation/application may have no knowledge link. Preserve the existing node path and human content when proposing additions.

A cropped-away original caption must survive completely beside the image: title, caption, abbreviations, symbols, exclusions and footnotes, with source location and continuation ownership. Retain immediate reading context in the crop and retain an untranscribed caption in the image. English preserves original wording; Chinese translates faithfully. Put analyst explanation, unit interpretation and suspected corrections in a separate labelled Reading note / 阅读说明 paragraph. Preserve the original discrepancy rather than editing it into apparent agreement.

The 2026-10-23 user correction requires direct functional Obsidian delivery, with no separate prose-only reader draft. Keep controlled Writer/Editor JSON and checkpoints as internal records. Preserve figures, internal navigation, knowledge links and meaningful back-links in the final note; missing functionality is not cured by a textual pass. A specifically requested style preview is a separate one-off example, not the normal delivery path or native acceptance.

For new configured readings, apply only language-appropriate optional guidance selected and frozen by `prepareConfiguredReading`; Humanizer and stss are not mandatory when disabled. Existing bindings retain their original prompt bytes. Any selected guidance acts during the existing Writer or Translation call. Improve natural phrasing without removing independent scientific content, numeric conditions, attribution or consequential uncertainty. Preserve Obsidian markup and source targets. No additional style reviewer, score, rewrite loop or model call is introduced. Readability remains part of the existing overall review. Integrator supplies the module scope and registered resources. The source-language Writer authors the complete module/subsection structure; Translation keeps that structure and its placements, purposeful Markdown and resource placements; Controller remapping of existing paragraphs cannot replace this work. New configured source-translation bindings pass the accepted source_view, terminology and labels to Translation, which preserves meaning and structure with natural target-language expression. The direction follows the paper's actual language, the same language that routes original/translation notes. The renderer resolves the registered references and resources; see [the interface and preview exporter](../../../../docs/apa-v3/reading-first-obsidian.md). It restores relative navigation, visuals, design–finding links, shared candidates and managed human-area protection. Those implementation capabilities do not themselves certify native Obsidian display.

For new unbound `reading-first-1.0`, render and merge the complete bilingual candidate first, then review that final representation once. Use [the active contract](../../../../prompts/v3/reading-first-contracts.md). Writers use the `writer-modules-1.0` module/section and placement contract without per-character reverse-grounding or every-target parity records. Keep concise academic headings, original abstracts and complete DOI labels. Important tables must be displayed in a readable orientation with headers and footnotes; necessary page, visual and knowledge references must be actual links.

Judge whether available important material is explained, whether research logic and method-to-result connections are understandable, and whether both languages preserve the same consequential meaning. Use the sixteen topics to notice omissions, not to require item-by-item bilingual locations or sign-offs. Main conclusions, crucial design and conclusion-changing numbers/formulas/table relations receive targeted source checks. Ordinary format/citation-style imperfections or unreviewed ordinary details do not block.

Within that same review, assess whether the hierarchy and numbering expose the argument, highlights identify consequential information, and methods/concepts are independently findable. A reader should grasp priorities without reading every sentence. Treat essential missing navigation/catalogue or misleading summaries as actionable presentation defects; route them to Reading Editor or renderer, without another scientific audit. Programmatic file/anchor existence and marker counts do not prove that jumps work in Obsidian. Record actual native observations separately; a user-rejected native result cannot inherit a textual pass.

A material omission, broken argument, wrong critical fact or important bilingual meaning difference requires owner-directed repair. Missing required modules, unreadable important tables and missing essential navigation also require repair; ordinary stylistic preferences remain minor. At most one localized recheck examines repaired material issues and actual affected context; do not reread every source or rerun all science roles. Retain unresolved material problems if that budget is exhausted. New changed final bytes cannot inherit an old verdict blindly; preserve revision/ownership binding without inventing an extra model pass.

The overall review includes rendered shared knowledge pages, paper-specific applications, inventory links and important source assets alongside both paper views. Readability in the paper does not compensate for an empty or incorrect shared node. Renderer-only repairs preserve accepted Writer/Editor bytes and use the existing bounded local-review branch documented in [execution-and-recovery.md](execution-and-recovery.md#route-a-repair-to-its-owner); they do not imply another full review.

Actual Obsidian pages/assets/navigation observations remain distinct from textual review. Native presentation is `not_run` without actual observations, or partial when coverage is partial; sampled success identifies exactly what was observed. The overall text review does not claim native acceptance or publication permission. Existing already-bound P22/P22.1 modes retain their own historical per-item/parity rules and original scope.
