## Recover the reader's requirements

The user-confirmed APA priority is research design and reasoning first, methods/concepts/measures second, results third. Organize by research question and explain why each design choice and step is needed, how inputs/outputs connect and what the evidence establishes; methods enable this understanding and results test it. This is an explanatory priority, not a length ratio or permission to omit results, conditions or any of the16 obligations. See the [current content requirements](../../../../docs/apa-v3/current-contracts.md).

Carry forward the accepted V2/V2.5 and later reading goals when changing a V3 page: explain the research problem, design reasons, data/steps, intermediate outputs, findings, interpretation and limits as a connected argument. Distinguish method principles from this paper's implementation. Explain necessary concepts in context; spelling out an acronym is not an explanation.

Preserve established managed identities and independent Personal Notes; use the sixteen reading topics as a content guide, without per-topic signing. The2026-10-20 user revision permits omitting or minimizing the visible Evidence, Relation Ledger and Coverage Report modules while retaining their complete retrievable records and consequential limitations in the reading flow. An overview guides entry; it does not replace the complete analysis. Do not convert every semantic unit into a separate heading/card or expose all role opinions. Important disagreements, author/analyst attribution and consequential uncertainty stay understandable in the reading flow.

## Preserve meaning while improving display

The inventory lists each essential method, measure or concept with its supported principle and actual application in this study. Keep contextual explanations too. Overview and takeaway text must preserve consequential conditions and uncertainty. Exact emphasis spans must avoid links, formulas, code, existing bold and overlapping fragments. Nature/Humanizer's objection to empty labels does not prohibit meaningful bold, numbered hierarchy, tables, lists or callouts. Reading usefulness takes precedence over mechanically restrained prose formatting.

One independent method, concept or chart type means one identity and one physical bilingual knowledge file. Preserve registered paths and human content. New filenames use clear Chinese canonical names without paper IDs, hashes or language suffixes. Both paper languages navigate to the same file's respective language section. Share formulas, figures, sources and the paper-application index within that file. In a standalone preview, render the existing node's merged candidate at its registered path. Keep historical candidates without using them as competing new entry points.

Configured readings keep `configured-reading-2.2` and freeze the separate `reading-editor-1.4` presentation contract when Editor is selected. Methods provides the generic teaching material: definition, principle, formulas/symbols, a clearly labelled example where useful, applicability, limitations and common confusions. The paper's inventory explains why and how this study uses the method and what that means for interpreting its results. For section-specific writing, consult only the relevant entries of the [module reference](../../../../docs/apa-v3/scientific-writing-modules.md). ## Review the final delivered representation

Shared cards use supported conventional names. The paper may use a more specific application heading, and an inventory entry with substantive explanation or application may have no knowledge link. The renderer can reuse a unique match against a committed node by exact name, normalized name, English name or registered alias. If the target changes before publication, deterministic rebase checks the paper's original scientific prose, the node contribution and the mapped link targets. Other runs' planned new nodes are hints for the renderer. Publication resolves against the latest committed registration.

When names or definitions suggest multiple or similar targets, local BM25 surfaces definition comparisons. The main session reads the relevant definitions and records a draft recommendation to merge, cross-link or retain the nodes. An open recommendation can accompany a publication that creates its own node. If a reviewed reuse has become ambiguous, pause for exact approval of the changed write list. An existing-node merge requires an approved transaction that preserves the prior text, links, aliases and registration for a compensating transaction. Old-page cleanup requires its own approval. See the [publication bridge](../../../../docs/apa-v3/reading-first-publication.md).

A cropped-away original caption must survive completely beside the image: title, caption, abbreviations, symbols, exclusions and footnotes, with source location and continuation ownership. Retain immediate reading context in the crop and retain an untranscribed caption in the image. English preserves original wording; Chinese translates faithfully. Put analyst explanation, unit interpretation and suspected corrections in a separate labelled Reading note / 阅读说明 paragraph. Preserve the original discrepancy rather than editing it into apparent agreement.

The 2026-10-23 user correction requires direct functional Obsidian delivery, with no separate prose-only reader draft. Keep controlled Writer/Editor JSON and checkpoints as internal records. Preserve figures, internal navigation, knowledge links and meaningful back-links in the final note; missing functionality is not cured by a textual pass. A specifically requested style preview is a separate one-off example, not the normal delivery path or native acceptance.

For new configured readings, apply only language-appropriate optional guidance selected and frozen by `prepareConfiguredReading`; Humanizer and stss are not mandatory when disabled. Any selected guidance acts during the existing Writer or Translation call. Improve natural phrasing without removing independent scientific content, numeric conditions, attribution or consequential uncertainty. Preserve Obsidian markup and source targets. No additional style reviewer, score, rewrite loop or model call is introduced. Readability remains part of the existing overall review. Integrator supplies the module scope and registered resources. The source-language Writer authors the complete module/subsection structure; Translation keeps that structure and its placements, purposeful Markdown and resource placements; Controller remapping of existing paragraphs cannot replace this work. New configured source-translation bindings pass the accepted source_view, terminology and labels to Translation, which preserves meaning and structure with natural target-language expression. The direction follows the paper's actual language, the same language that routes original/translation notes. The renderer resolves the registered references and resources; see [the interface and preview exporter](../../../../docs/apa-v3/reading-first-obsidian.md). Judge whether available important material is explained, whether research logic and method-to-result connections are understandable, and whether both languages preserve the same consequential meaning.
